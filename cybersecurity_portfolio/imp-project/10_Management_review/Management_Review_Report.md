# 6. Opportunities for Improvement

1. Formalize vendor reassessment activities.
2. Conduct incident response exercises.
3. Increase employee security awareness.
4. Improve security monitoring capabilities.
5. Expand risk reporting dashboards.

---

# 7. Management Decisions

Management agreed to:

* Continue implementation of the ISMS.
* Complete all planned risk treatment activities.
* Perform annual internal audits.
* Conduct annual management reviews.
* Increase focus on vendor risk management.
* Schedule incident response testing.

---

# 8. Resource Requirements

The following resources were approved:

* Security awareness training budget.
* Security monitoring improvements.
* Vendor assessment activities.
* Annual penetration testing.

---

# 9. Conclusion

Senior management concludes that the ISMS is operating effectively and remains aligned with business objectives.

The organization will continue improving its information security capabilities through ongoing risk management, control implementation, and compliance activities.
