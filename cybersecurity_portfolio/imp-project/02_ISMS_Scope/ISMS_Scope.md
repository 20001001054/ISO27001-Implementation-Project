# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the scope of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) implemented within CredSecure Finance Ltd in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

The ISMS is established to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets supporting the company's business operations and services.

---

# 2. Organization Overview

CredSecure Finance Ltd is a financial technology (FinTech) organization providing invoice financing and working capital solutions to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

The company delivers its services through cloud-based platforms, web applications, and mobile applications while processing sensitive customer, financial, and operational information.

---

# 3. Scope Statement

The Information Security Management System (ISMS) applies to all information assets, employees, contractors, business processes, applications, databases, cloud infrastructure, and supporting technologies involved in the delivery of invoice financing services by CredSecure Finance Ltd.

The scope includes all systems and processes used to collect, process, store, transmit, and manage customer and business information.

---

# 4. Business Functions Included in Scope

The following business functions are included within the ISMS:

* Customer Onboarding
* Know Your Customer (KYC) Verification
* Invoice Financing Operations
* Payment Processing
* Customer Support Services
* Software Development
* IT Operations
* Risk Management
* Compliance Management
* Vendor Management

---

# 5. Information Assets Included in Scope

The following information assets are included:

* Customer Personal Information
* Customer Financial Information
* Invoice Records
* Transaction Data
* Employee Information
* Vendor Information
* Source Code Repositories
* Security Logs
* Audit Records
* Business Documentation

---

# 6. Technology Assets Included in Scope

The following technology assets are included:

### Applications

* Customer Web Portal
* Mobile Application
* Internal Administration Portal

### Infrastructure

* AWS EC2 Instances
* AWS RDS Databases
* AWS S3 Storage
* AWS IAM
* AWS CloudTrail
* Backup Systems
* Monitoring Systems

### Development Environment

* Source Code Repositories
* CI/CD Pipelines
* Development and Testing Environments

---

# 7. Personnel Included in Scope

The ISMS applies to:

* Full-Time Employees
* Contractors
* Temporary Staff
* Third-Party Service Providers with System Access
* Management Personnel

---

# 8. Physical Locations Included in Scope

The ISMS covers:

* Corporate Headquarters (Dublin, Ireland)
* Development Office (India)
* Remote Working Environments
* Cloud Infrastructure Hosted in AWS

---

# 9. Exclusions

The following are excluded from the ISMS scope:

* Personal devices not used for company business
* Third-party systems outside the organization's management control
* Publicly available marketing content and public website information

---

# 10. ISMS Objectives

The objectives of the ISMS are:

1. Protect customer and financial information from unauthorized access.
2. Ensure compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
3. Identify and reduce information security risks.
4. Support secure and reliable business operations.
5. Improve employee security awareness.
6. Maintain business continuity and operational resilience.

---

# 11. Applicable Legal and Regulatory Requirements

The ISMS considers the following requirements:

* ISO/IEC 27001:2022
* General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
* Irish Data Protection Legislation
* Contractual Security Obligations
* Industry Best Practices

---

# 12. Scope Approval

This ISMS scope shall be reviewed annually or whenever significant changes occur to the organization, technology environment, regulatory obligations, or business operations.
