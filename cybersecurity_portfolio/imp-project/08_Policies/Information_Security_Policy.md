---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish the framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets belonging to CredSecure Finance Ltd.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

* Employees
* Contractors
* Third-Party Service Providers
* Information Systems
* Cloud Infrastructure
* Applications
* Data Assets

---

# 3. Policy Statement

CredSecure Finance Ltd is committed to:

* Protecting customer information.
* Managing information security risks.
* Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.
* Implementing security controls aligned with ISO 27001.
* Maintaining business continuity and operational resilience.

---

# 4. Information Security Objectives

* Protect sensitive information.
* Reduce cyber risk exposure.
* Ensure regulatory compliance.
* Improve security awareness.
* Support secure business operations.

---

# 5. Roles and Responsibilities

## Management

Provide support and resources for information security activities.

## Employees

Follow security policies and report security incidents.

## Security Team

Monitor risks, implement controls, and manage incidents.

---

# 6. Policy Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action and corrective measures.

---

# 7. Review

This policy shall be reviewed annually or following significant business changes.
