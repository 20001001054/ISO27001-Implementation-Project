# Vendor Security Policy

## Purpose

To manage information security risks associated with third-party vendors and service providers.

---

## Vendor Assessments

All vendors shall undergo a security assessment before onboarding.

---

## Security Requirements

Vendors must:

* Protect customer information.
* Comply with applicable regulations.
* Notify the organization of security incidents.
* Support audit requests when contractually required.

---

## Periodic Reviews

Critical vendors shall be reviewed annually.

---

## Contractual Security Clauses

Contracts shall include:

* Data protection requirements
* Security obligations
* Incident notification requirements
* Termination provisions
