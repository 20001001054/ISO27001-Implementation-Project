# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

To ensure security incidents are detected, reported, investigated, and resolved effectively.

---

## Incident Lifecycle

### Identification

Identify suspicious activity.

### Reporting

Report incidents immediately to the Security Team.

### Containment

Limit the impact of the incident.

### Eradication

Remove the root cause.

### Recovery

Restore affected services.

### Lessons Learned

Document findings and improvements.

---

## Severity Levels

| Level    | Description             |
| -------- | ----------------------- |
| Low      | Minor impact            |
| Medium   | Limited business impact |
| High     | Significant disruption  |
| Critical | Severe business impact  |
