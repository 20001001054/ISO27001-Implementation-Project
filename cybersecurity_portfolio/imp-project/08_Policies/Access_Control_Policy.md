# Access Control Policy

## Purpose

To ensure that access to information systems is granted only to authorized individuals based on business requirements.

---

## Policy Requirements

### User Access Management

* All users must have unique accounts.
* Shared accounts are prohibited.
* Access requests require management approval.

### Least Privilege

* Users shall receive the minimum access necessary to perform their duties.

### Privileged Access

* Administrative accounts shall be restricted and monitored.
* Privileged access reviews shall be conducted quarterly.

### Access Reviews

* User access rights shall be reviewed at least every six months.

### Access Removal

* Access must be removed immediately following employee termination or role changes.
