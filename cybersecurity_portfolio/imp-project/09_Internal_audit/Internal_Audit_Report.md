# 1. Audit Objective

To evaluate whether the Information Security Management System (ISMS) has been implemented and maintained in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.

---

# 2. Audit Scope

The audit covered:

* ISMS Scope
* Asset Management
* Risk Management
* Risk Treatment Activities
* Information Security Policies
* Access Control Processes
* Vendor Management Processes
* Incident Management Processes

---

# 3. Audit Methodology

The audit included:

* Document review
* Policy review
* Risk register review
* Interviews with process owners
* Control effectiveness assessment
* Sampling of evidence records

# 5. Audit Findings

## Finding 1

### Type

Observation

### Area

Vendor Management

### Description

Vendor security reviews have been defined within the Vendor Security Policy; however, evidence of annual vendor reassessments was not available.

### Recommendation

Implement a formal vendor review schedule and maintain assessment records.

---

## Finding 2

### Type

Observation

### Area

Incident Response

### Description

An Incident Response Policy exists, but no incident response tabletop exercise has been performed.

### Recommendation

Conduct annual incident response testing and document lessons learned.

---

# 6. Positive Practices

The audit identified the following strengths:

* Well-maintained asset inventory
* Comprehensive risk register
* Defined risk treatment process
* Clear policy framework
* Management commitment to information security

---

# 7. Audit Conclusion

The ISMS is generally operating effectively and demonstrates substantial alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.

No major non-conformities were identified.

Several improvement opportunities were noted and should be addressed through corrective actions.
