# 1. Purpose

This methodology defines the process used to identify, analyze, evaluate, and prioritize information security risks within CredSecure Finance Ltd.

The objective is to ensure that risks are assessed consistently and treated according to the organization's risk appetite.

---

# 2. Risk Assessment Process

The risk assessment process consists of the following steps:

1. Identify Assets
2. Identify Threats
3. Identify Vulnerabilities
4. Assess Likelihood
5. Assess Impact
6. Calculate Risk Score
7. Determine Risk Level
8. Select Risk Treatment

---

# 3. Likelihood Criteria

| Score | Likelihood  | Description                    |
| ----- | ----------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1     | Rare        | Highly unlikely to occur       |
| 2     | Unlikely    | Could occur but not expected   |
| 3     | Possible    | May occur occasionally         |
| 4     | Likely      | Expected to occur periodically |
| 5     | Very Likely | Expected to occur frequently   |

---

# 4. Impact Criteria

| Score | Impact     | Description                                    |
| ----- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Negligible | Minimal business impact                        |
| 2     | Low        | Minor operational disruption                   |
| 3     | Moderate   | Noticeable business impact                     |
| 4     | High       | Significant financial or operational impact    |
| 5     | Critical   | Severe business, legal, or reputational damage |

---

# 5. Risk Scoring Formula

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

Minimum Score = 1

Maximum Score = 25

---

# 6. Risk Rating Matrix

| Score   | Risk Level |
| ------- | ---------- |
| 1 – 5   | Low        |
| 6 – 10  | Medium     |
| 11 – 15 | High       |
| 16 – 25 | Critical   |

---

# 7. Risk Treatment Options

## Mitigate

Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact.

## Transfer

Transfer risk through insurance, contracts, or third parties.

## Accept

Accept the risk where treatment costs exceed benefits.

## Avoid

Eliminate the activity causing the risk.

---

# 8. Risk Appetite

CredSecure Finance Ltd has:

* Low tolerance for risks affecting customer data
* Low tolerance for regulatory compliance failures
* Low tolerance for financial fraud risks
* Moderate tolerance for minor operational disruptions

---

# 9. Risk Acceptance Criteria

| Risk Level | Action                       |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- |
| Low        | Acceptable                   |
| Medium     | Monitor and review           |
| High       | Treatment required           |
| Critical   | Immediate treatment required |

---

# 10. Review Frequency

Risk assessments shall be reviewed:

* Annually
* Following major system changes
* Following significant security incidents
* Following major regulatory changes
