Organization Overview

Company Name: CredSecure Finance Ltd

Industry: Financial Technology (FinTech)

Headquarters: Dublin, Ireland

Operations: Ireland and India

Employees: 150

Annual Customers: 50,000+

Business Description:

CredSecure Finance Ltd is a financial technology company that provides invoice financing and working capital solutions to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). The company operates a cloud-based platform where businesses can upload invoices, apply for financing, manage repayments, and monitor financial transactions.

The platform processes sensitive customer information, financial records, payment details, and business documents. Services are delivered through web and mobile applications hosted on AWS cloud infrastructure.
