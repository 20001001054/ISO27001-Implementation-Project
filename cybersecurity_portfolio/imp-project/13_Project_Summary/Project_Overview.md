# ISO 27001 Implementation and Cyber Risk Management Project

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the implementation of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for a fictional FinTech organization, CredSecure Finance Ltd.

The objective was to identify information assets, assess cybersecurity risks, implement risk treatment activities, map controls to ISO 27001 requirements, and establish governance processes to support information security and regulatory compliance.

---

# Project Scope

The ISMS covers:

* Customer-facing applications
* Cloud infrastructure hosted on AWS
* Customer and financial data
* Internal business processes
* Employees and third-party vendors

---

# Key Activities Performed

## ISMS Establishment

* Defined ISMS scope
* Performed organizational context analysis
* Identified interested parties and compliance obligations

## Asset Management

* Created asset inventory
* Classified information assets
* Assessed confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements

## Risk Assessment

* Developed risk assessment methodology
* Established risk scoring criteria
* Identified and assessed 30 cybersecurity risks

## Risk Treatment

* Prioritized critical and high risks
* Developed risk treatment plans
* Calculated residual risk levels

## ISO 27001 Control Mapping

* Created Statement of Applicability (SoA)
* Mapped risks to applicable ISO 27001 controls
* Assigned control owners and implementation status

## Governance and Compliance

* Developed information security policies
* Established vendor security requirements
* Defined incident response processes

## Internal Audit

* Conducted internal ISMS audit
* Identified observations and improvement opportunities
* Established corrective actions

## Management Review

* Presented ISMS performance metrics
* Reviewed risk treatment progress
* Recommended future improvements
